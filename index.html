My process

I like keeping the design process practical: understand the problem first, reduce ambiguity, test the direction, and only then polish the interface or move it into code.

01

Discover

Understand the brief, user needs, constraints and what success should look like.

02

Define

Organise flows, prioritise information and turn research into a focused product direction.

03

Design

Move from wireframes into visual systems, interaction details and responsive screens.

04

Deliver

Prototype, test, refine and collaborate with development—or build the front end myself.
